Every founder-led company hits the same wall. Marketing works fine when you're small enough to hold the whole plan in your head — but growth surfaces problems that founder instinct alone can't solve.

## The signs

- Your team is executing tactics without a connecting strategy
- You're the bottleneck on every marketing decision, big or small
- Growth has plateaued despite more spend, more content, more campaigns

## What actually changes it

Senior marketing leadership doesn't mean more headcount — it means someone accountable for turning tactical activity into a growth-led system. That's the gap a fractional CMO fills.
