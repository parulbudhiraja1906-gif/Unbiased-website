The full-time-vs-fractional question usually gets framed as a cost decision. It's really a risk and timing decision.

## Cost

A full-time CMO is a fixed, long-term commitment. A fractional CMO scales with the engagement — you pay for the function, not the headcount.

## Speed

Hiring a full-time CMO can take months. A fractional engagement can start in weeks, with senior judgment applied from day one.

## When full-time makes sense

Once the function is proven and the roadmap is set, a full-time hire can take over execution — often the same plan a fractional CMO built.
